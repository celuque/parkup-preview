How the afternoon works

What’s on

Ten or so Sunshine Coast businesses set up along the bar, each one trading live on the same commerce stack Techweave builds for a living. These are actual shops, running for the afternoon.

  1. 01.

    Grab a bag at the door and scan your pass on the way in.

  2. 02.

    Stop at a stall and buy something, or just ask them to ring one through.

  3. 03.

    Watch the order land in the inventory system behind it, live.

  4. 04.

    Scan your pass again to release that stall’s free item.

  5. 05.

    Drop it in the bag, then do it again at the next one.

Who’s coming

If you run a business on this coast, you should be in the room.

  • Makers

    About 1,450 manufacturing businesses operate on this coast, employing close to 10,000 people.

  • Retailers

    Ecommerce brands with a shop, a website, or both.

  • Wholesalers

    Businesses selling into other businesses.

  • The curious

    Anyone wondering what’s actually possible now.

  • Coast wide

    From Coolum to Noosa, Maroochydore out to the hinterland.

  • No tech needed

    You don’t need to be technical, or already use any of this.

The honest version

The people who build these systems are right there. So are the Sunshine Coast businesses already running them, and they’ll give you the honest version. Then grab another beer and talk to whoever is useful.

At around 5pm we stop for four or five minutes. Tim Kelly from the Manufacturing Ecosystem Forum talks about why manufacturing on this coast matters, and Shopify says a few words. It’s the only moment all afternoon you need to stand still for.

Who’s behind it

Hosted by Techweave, the Sunshine Coast and Sydney team who build and run these systems for Australian retailers and manufacturers.

Shopify and Cin7 are our lead partners. LiteCard runs your pass and your first drink. The Manufacturing Ecosystem Forum Sunshine Coast is helping us fill the room.

Questions

How much is it?

Nothing. Your first drink and the food are on us. Anything after that you buy at the bar like everyone else.

Do I have to stay the whole time?

No. Most people will do an hour. Come and go as it suits.

Do I need to already use Shopify, or Cin7, or any of this?

No. Plenty of people coming don’t. That’s rather the point.

Is this a sales pitch?

There’s no pitch and no stage presentation. The people who sell these products are in the room and happy to talk if you want them to. Afterwards you’ll hear from us once, and from any partner you’ve ticked on the registration form. Tick nothing and you’ll hear from nobody.

Is there a dress code?

No. Come off the factory floor or out of the shop. Most people will have.

Can I bring someone?

Yes, please. Register them separately so they get their own pass.

Is there parking?

Street parking is available. It’s an active business park, so please don’t park in the tenants’ spots.

Will there be speeches?

One short one, at around 5pm. Tim Kelly from the Manufacturing Ecosystem Forum and a few words from Shopify. Four or five minutes, no microphone. That’s it.
