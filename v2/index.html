Questions

How much is it?

Nothing. Your first drink and the food are on us. Anything after that you buy at the bar like everyone else.

Do I have to stay the whole time?

No. Come and go as it suits, and pick your hour by the talk you want to catch. They run at 4, 5 and 6.

Do I need to already use Shopify, or Cin7, or any of this?

No. We want both kinds of business in the room: the ones already running Shopify or Cin7, and the ones wondering whether it would make their week easier.

Is this a sales pitch?

Partly, and we’d rather say so up front. Techweave works with Shopify, Cin7 and these partners every day, and we’d like you to see how much simpler they can make running a business. The three short talks are there to make the afternoon worth your time. The rest of it is yours, with no pressure to buy anything. Afterwards you’ll hear from us once, and from any partner you’ve ticked on the form.

Is there a dress code?

No. Come off the factory floor or out of the shop. Most people will have.

Can I bring someone?

Yes, please. Register them separately so they get their own pass.

Is there parking?

Street parking is available. It’s an active business park, so please don’t park in the tenants’ spots.

Will there be speeches?

Three talks, at 4pm, 5pm and 6pm, ten to fifteen minutes each, from Shopify, the Manufacturing Excellence Forum and Cin7. That’s the only structure to the afternoon.
